DBA Concentration Options

Four concentrations, each combining doctoral research with hands-on practical training from Day 1.

Hospitality Management

A specialization in hospitality management prepares you for work in international strategy and leadership, cultivating foundational skills for consulting and corporate positions. Internships provide a hands-on learning environment where you sharpen management skills and build business relationships in your community — real-world experience that strengthens your resume and showcases your skills to future employers.

Management

The management concentration develops executive-level leaders ready for an AI-driven landscape. You will study contemporary leadership theory, organizational strategy, and change management, then apply that research directly to the organizations you work with. It is the broadest pathway for professionals targeting senior leadership, consulting, or entrepreneurship.

Information & Data Science

Designed for students who want to lead the data revolution, this specialization builds advanced skills in data science, analytics, and intelligent automation. You will learn to contribute to your field through quantitative analysis while examining the ethical and societal considerations of data usage. Guided practical training is available from Day 1, so you sharpen your skills in a real business setting.

Marketing

The marketing specialization supplies broad knowledge across diverse industries — how marketing drives organizational strategy in both business and politics. Coursework spans human behavior, social influencing, marketing research, and data analytics, while internship-focused training connects you with prospective employers and adds practical value to your resume.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the DBA program start?

The program runs on an eight-week term structure with intakes in January, March, May, July, August, and October. If you are finishing a master’s or OPT, register 3–6 months in advance so your start date lines up. You can review the academic calendar to plan ahead.

Do I need an MBA to apply?

No. The DBA accepts graduates from accredited master’s programs — the MBA-to-DBA route is simply the most common path among our students. Because the doctorate is a higher degree level than any master’s, moving up also qualifies you for a new period of OPT after you graduate.

How long does it take to complete the DBA?

The DBA is a 52-unit program that most students complete in approximately 3 years, depending on pace and dissertation progress. Students who transfer 9 eligible units complete 43 credits.

Can I work while I study?

Yes. The program includes Day 1 CPT, so practical training is part of the curriculum from your first term and continues throughout the program. The hybrid executive format — on campus every 8 weeks — is designed so you can hold a professional role while you study, and you qualify for OPT when you finish.

What grants and financial aid are available?

The EduConnect/Alliant grant covers up to $15,000, prorated per credit. Combined with transfer units, estimated program fees come to $46,612 — payable through an interest-free plan ($375 registration, $4,000 down, then $650 every two weeks for 3 years).

Private and public scholarships, loans, grants, federal work-study, and military aid may also be available: Scholarship opportunities | Financial Options Guide.
