Day 1 CPT Programs

Start working from the first day

Alliant University offers an opportunity for its students to begin their Curricular Practical Training (CPT) right from the first day. This is also referred to as Day 1 CPT. During the initial academic year, students have the option to work and study part-time or full-time. In the subsequent year after graduation, they can work full-time as part of Optional Practical Training (OPT). This structure allows students to gain work experience in their internships while pursuing their American degree.

CPT & OPT Work Eligibility

Gain CPT and OPT Work Eligibility

Day 1 CPT (Curricular Practical Training) is offered in all programs, allowing eligible international students to begin working in their field from the start of their program. This gives immediate access to practical work experience, ensuring that your education is complemented by professional development right from the beginning.

EduConnect USA provides full support throughout your work and study journey. Our dedicated team helps you find suitable placements, provides career coaching, and ensures you meet all necessary visa and compliance requirements. We guide you through the entire process, from job search and resume building to interview preparation and work authorization. The benefits of participating in our Work and Study Programs are clear: you will earn an income to support your education, build a professional network, and gain U.S. work experience that strengthens your resume and makes you more competitive in the global job market.

See More on Work Placement
CPT and OPT are federal work authorizations with specific eligibility rules. Availability of Day 1 CPT, work hours, and STEM OPT depends on your program, your status, and authorization by your Designated School Official (DSO) in line with USCIS and SEVP regulations. Our team confirms your eligibility before you begin any employment.
