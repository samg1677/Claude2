Good to Know

MS in Data Analytics FAQs

What kind of jobs can I get with an MS in Data Analytics?

You can build a career in many specialized fields — data scientist, data analyst, business intelligence consultant, data architect, big data engineer, analytics project manager, and quantitative modeler are all excellent options.

How do I make my tuition payments?

We divide program costs into small, interest-free payments made roughly every two weeks over two years. After your down payment, our system automatically splits the remaining balance so you can pay manageable installments while you study and work.

How does the hybrid schedule work?

You complete most coursework online and come to campus twice each 8-week term — once in Week 1 and once in Week 8. You can attend these sessions in Los Angeles or San Diego.

What concentrations are offered?

The Executive MSDA includes two concentrations. Healthcare Analytics focuses on interpreting clinical data, and Informatics centers on enterprise data systems and workflows. Both prepare you for data-driven roles in the healthcare and tech industries.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The Executive MSDA is a 33-credit program that typically takes 12 to 16 months to complete. After you graduate, you gain STEM OPT eligibility, allowing you to keep working in the U.S. for up to three additional years.

Can I work while I study?

Yes. As a STEM-designated program with Day 1 CPT, you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start of your program, then continue on STEM OPT for up to 3 years after graduation.
