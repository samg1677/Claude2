Executive MS in Data Analytics

A STEM master's designed around your visa and your job

The Executive Master of Science in Data Analytics is delivered by an accredited California institution and built for international professionals who cannot afford to stop working for two years. It is 33 credits, takes 12–16 months, and runs in a hybrid format — coursework online, on campus only twice per 8-week term in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Because the degree is STEM-designated and Day 1 CPT eligible, you can begin paid U.S. employment in your very first term and continue working on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate. Tuition is paid in small, interest-free installments, and a $5,500 grant is available.

How an 8-week term works

Built around a full-time job, not against it

Each term runs eight weeks. You are on campus at the beginning and the end — the rest is online, on your schedule.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of jobs can I get with an MS in Data Analytics?
Graduates work as data scientists, data analysts, business intelligence consultants, data architects, big data engineers, project managers, and quantitative modelers across technology, healthcare, finance, and government.
How do I make my tuition payments?
Program costs are divided into small, interest-free installments paid roughly every two weeks over two years. After your down payment, the remaining balance is split automatically, so you pay manageable amounts while you study and work.
How does the hybrid schedule work?
Most coursework is completed online. You come to campus twice each 8-week term — in Week 1 and Week 8 — and those sessions can be attended in Los Angeles or San Diego.
What concentrations are offered?
Two: Healthcare Analytics, focused on interpreting clinical and patient data, and Informatics, focused on enterprise data systems, governance, and workflows.
How long does it take to complete the program?
The Executive MSDA is a 33-credit program that typically takes 12 to 16 months. After graduation, STEM OPT eligibility allows up to three additional years of U.S. work authorization.
Can I really work from my first term?
Yes. The program is Day 1 CPT eligible, so once your CPT is authorized you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start — and continue on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate.
