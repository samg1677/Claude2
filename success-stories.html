Case Study · Alliant University

Innovating education with a co-branded degree

EduConnect created a co-branded Executive MBA with Alliant University, designed to address the key challenges international students face. The goal was simple: high-quality education, flexible learning, and real-world experience, all at a reduced cost.

  • Lower tuition and grants: we lowered the tuition point and secured university grants, including a $6,000 grant toward tuition, plus an interest-free student payment plan.
  • Flexibility after Covid: we helped design a hybrid schedule that balances study, work, and life while aligning with federal regulations.
  • Practical experience: our network of industry partners provides CPT and OPT placement support, so students can start working from day one.

Case Study · Vatel

Expanding a global network in hospitality

Through our collaboration, the Vatel Institute of Hospitality Management was born, giving students the chance to study at Alliant's Los Angeles campus and earn an accredited US MBA alongside a Master's degree from France.

Our online marketing drove brand awareness through live webinars and targeted social campaigns, filling the program with talented students from around the world.

  • An accredited US MBA earned alongside a French Master's degree
  • Over 200 students enrolled from 23 different countries
  • Practical training at prestigious 4 and 5 star hotel properties

Case Study · International American University

Growing a global footprint through franchising

EduConnect USA played a pivotal role in expanding International American University by franchising its programs to international universities, establishing IAU Madrid and IAU Bordeaux, with more campuses on the horizon.

We matched IAU with reputable European institutions, negotiated the franchise agreements, and protected the integrity of the IAU brand every step of the way.

  • New campuses launched in Madrid and Bordeaux
  • Partner institutions matched for academic fit and quality
  • A repeatable model, with further expansion on the horizon
