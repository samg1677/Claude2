First Things First

What practical training actually means

Practical training is built into your academic program and gives you hands-on experience directly related to your field of study. Through a CPT-eligible placement you apply classroom learning in a real work setting while you continue your education, and the role itself counts as part of your curriculum.

That structure is what makes working from your first term possible, and it is why the placement has to genuinely connect to what you study. CareerConnect makes sure the roles we help you target and the offer you accept fit that requirement, so your work authorization stays clean from day one.

Placements that stay compliant

  • Roles genuinely tied to your field of study
  • Work that counts as part of your curriculum
  • SEVIS authorization coordinated with your DSO
  • An updated I-20 before your first day of work

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What is Curricular Practical Training (CPT)?

CPT is work authorization for F-1 students when the employment is an integral part of the curriculum, such as an internship, a co-op, or a required practical training component. Most students become eligible after one full academic year, though some graduate programs that require practical training from the start are exempt from the one-year wait. Your DSO must authorize the specific role in SEVIS and issue an updated I-20 before you begin working.

What is Optional Practical Training (OPT)?

OPT is work authorization that lets F-1 students work in their field of study, most commonly for 12 months after completing a degree, with a 24-month extension available for eligible STEM graduates. Unlike CPT, OPT is not tied to a specific course, and it requires an application to USCIS and an approved Employment Authorization Document before you can start work.

What is the difference between part-time and full-time CPT?

Part-time CPT is 20 hours per week or less, and full-time is anything above that. The distinction matters beyond your schedule: 12 months or more of full-time CPT at a degree level removes your eligibility for OPT at that same level, while part-time CPT never counts toward that limit. Decide how to balance the two with your DSO before your first authorization.
