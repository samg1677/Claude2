# Work & Travel / J-1 / Au Pair Instagram Account Directory

A curated directory of Instagram accounts covering U.S. cultural-exchange programs
(J-1 Summer Work Travel, Au Pair, Camp Counselor, internships) — including sending
agencies in a variety of countries whose participants come to the USA.

Machine-readable version: [`data/instagram_accounts.csv`](data/instagram_accounts.csv)
Profile scraper (bring your own login): [`scraper/`](scraper/)

Follower counts are approximate, as of research date (July 2026).

## Finding more accounts

- Hashtags: `#workandtravel`, `#workandtravelusa`, `#j1visa`, `#summerworktravel`,
  `#aupair`, `#aupairusa`, `#aupairlife`, `#aupairbrasil`, `#campcounselor`, `#bridgeusa`
- The official list of all designated J-1 sponsors (by program category) is at
  [j1visa.state.gov sponsor search](https://j1visa.state.gov/participants/how-to-apply/sponsor-search/) —
  most sponsors link their Instagram from their websites.
- Many agencies run separate accounts per country/city (see STB regionals above);
  check the "Related accounts" suggestions on any agency profile.
