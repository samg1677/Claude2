Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

What actually is “Day 1 CPT”?

Curricular Practical Training is off-campus work authorization tied to your field of study. In most programs you have to complete one full academic year before you can use it. In these programs, the curriculum is structured so CPT is available from your first term — which is why people call it Day 1 CPT.

It is authorized by the school’s Designated School Official and recorded on your I-20. It is not a separate visa and it is not a workaround; it is the standard F-1 CPT provision applied to a curriculum built around working professionals.

Do I have to leave the U.S. to change my status?

No. The entire change of status process is handled domestically through USCIS. You don’t visit a consulate and you don’t leave the country — you stay in the U.S. through the application, the approval and your enrollment.

How long does the change of status take?

Standard USCIS processing is typically 3–5 months. Premium processing is available for an additional fee and generally returns a decision in about 30 business days. Premium processing speeds up the answer; it doesn’t change the outcome. We recommend it when your current visa is close to expiring.

Can I keep my current job?

Usually yes, if the role relates to your field of study. If you already have an employer, we help with the transition and the paperwork on their side. If you don’t, our work placement team helps you find a paid position aligned with your degree so your CPT is put to use from the start.

Do I have to move to California?

No. The hybrid schedule means you can live and work in any U.S. state — our students are spread across most of them. The only requirement is attending the two in-person sessions each 8-week term at a California campus, Los Angeles or San Diego, whichever is easier for you.

What happens after I graduate?

You’re eligible for OPT, which is up to 12 months of post-graduation work authorization. If you graduate from one of the three STEM-designated master’s programs, you can apply for the 24-month STEM OPT extension on top of that — three years of work authorization in total.

Is the degree properly accredited?

Yes. Alliant University is accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission — the same regional accreditor as USC, UCLA and UC San Diego. All business programs additionally hold ACBSP accreditation.

What does it cost, honestly?

Eligible students receive a scholarship between $5,500 and $15,000 depending on the program, applied directly to tuition. The remaining balance is paid through an interest-free bi-weekly plan — no loans, no financing charges. An advisor gives you your exact numbers on the first call, before you apply.
