Who We Are

Your bridge to a U.S. education and career

EduConnect USA is a leading international student consultancy for students who want to work and study in the United States. We match you with accredited graduate programs, support your visa and change-of-status process, and connect you with American companies for internships and professional opportunities.

Our team brings deep experience in higher education and cultural exchange, so we can guide you through every step of the journey, from your first question to your first day on the job.

  • 15+years in higher education
  • 25+years in cultural exchange
Our Mission & Vision

Making a U.S. education accessible and stress-free

Our mission is to simplify the path for international students by providing affordable support at every stage. We guide students from program selection through enrollment, then help with relocation and career growth so they can thrive in the United States.
