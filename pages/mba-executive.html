Already in the United States?

A clear path to stay - whatever visa you hold today

This is the part most students don't realize is possible. If you are currently in the U.S. on a J-1, B-1/B-2, or F-1 visa, enrolling in the MBA gives you a defined route to continue with your current employer or transition to a new one, without leaving the country to do it.

J-1 Visa

Change of status from J-1

Finishing an exchange or training program? A change of status into F-1 student status lets you stay, start the MBA, and keep building your U.S. career. See the J-1 change of status process.

B-1 / B-2

Visitor to student

Already in the U.S. as a visitor and want to turn the trip into a degree and a career? We handle the change of status into F-1 so you can enroll and begin working on CPT from your first term.

F-1 Transfer

SEVIS transfer from another school

Already on an F-1 at another institution, or coming to the end of OPT? A SEVIS transfer moves your record over with no gap in status, and your CPT eligibility begins with your first term here.

Timing is the whole game. Whichever route applies to you, start the conversation early - three to six months before your current status or OPT ends is the window that keeps everything seamless. An EduConnect advisor will map your specific timeline in one call. Learn about our U.S. visa support.

How an 8-week term works

Built around a full-time job, not against it

Each term runs eight weeks. You are on campus at the beginning and the end - the rest is online, on your schedule, while you keep working on CPT.

Week 1

On campus

Open the term in person with faculty and your cohort in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Weeks 2–7

Online & working

Complete coursework online around your job. This is where the executive format earns its keep: what you study on a Tuesday night is something you can apply at work on Wednesday morning, and your CPT employment is part of the education, not a distraction from it.

Week 8

On campus

Close the term in person with presentations, assessments, and cohort sessions.

Los Angeles & San Diego Six terms per year Full-time enrollment keeps your F-1 status active throughout.

You do not have to move to California

This is the detail that changes everything for students who already have a job, a lease, or a family somewhere else. The hybrid schedule means you can live and work in any U.S. state. The only requirement is that you attend the two in-person sessions each 8-week term at a California campus - Los Angeles or San Diego, whichever is easier for you. See campus locations.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Executive MBA take?
Twelve months. It is an Executive MBA delivered on a hybrid schedule through EduConnect USA's partnership with Alliant University, so you finish a full U.S. master's degree in a single year while continuing to work. That is half the time of a conventional two-year MBA - and half the time paying tuition.
Can I work while I study?
Yes, and this is the reason it is our most sought-after program. The MBA is Day 1 CPT eligible, so you can be authorized to work off campus in your field from your first term rather than waiting out a full academic year. After you graduate, OPT extends your time working in the United States.
I'm already in the U.S. on a J-1, B-1/B-2 or F-1 visa. Can I move into this MBA?
Yes - this is one of the most common paths into the program. Current J-1, B-1/B-2, and F-1 visa holders can transition into the MBA through a change of status or a SEVIS transfer, giving you a clear route to stay in the U.S. and either continue with your current employer or move to a new one. Start the process 3–6 months before your current status ends. J-1 change of status | U.S. visa support.
Do I have to live in California?
No. The hybrid schedule lets you live and work in any U.S. state. The only requirement is attending the two in-person sessions each 8-week term at a California campus - Los Angeles or San Diego, whichever suits you. See campus locations.
Which concentrations can I choose from?
Four: Management & Decision Sciences, Information & Technology, Marketing & Digital Trends, and Hospitality Management. Every one carries the same 12-month timeline, the same hybrid schedule, and the same Day 1 CPT eligibility - only the specialty changes.
What does the MBA cost?
Eligible students receive a $6,000 grant applied directly to tuition, and the remaining balance can be paid through an interest-free bi-weekly payment plan. There are no loans and no financing charges. An advisor will give you your exact numbers in your free consultation.
When can I start?
There are six start dates every year: January, March, May, July, August, and October. If you are finishing OPT or another program, register 3–6 months ahead so your start date lines up with no gap in status.
Is the degree properly accredited?
Yes. Alliant University is accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission - the same regional accreditor as USC, UCLA, and UC San Diego. All business programs additionally hold ACBSP accreditation, a specialized standard for business schools. About Alliant University.
What if I don't have a job lined up yet?
You are covered either way. If you already have an employer, we help you keep that role while you study. If you don't, our work and internship placement support helps you find a paid position aligned with your degree, so your CPT is put to use from the start. Meet CareerConnect.
