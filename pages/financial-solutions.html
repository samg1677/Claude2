How it works

Three layers that make a U.S. degree affordable

Most students assume the choice is savings or a loan. It isn't. The grant lowers the number, the payment plan splits what's left, and the income from your work and study program covers the payments as they come.

1

The grant reduces your tuition

Your grant is applied directly to what you owe - money off the top, not a loan you repay later. Depending on your program that's $5,500, $6,000, or up to $15,000 before you pay a cent.

2

The balance is split, interest-free

Instead of paying large sums upfront, the remaining balance is broken into smaller payments made every two weeks. No loans. No interest. No financing charges.

3

Your income covers the payments

With an income from the work and study programs, you can meet your financial obligations while pursuing your education - aligning your tuition payments with what you actually earn.

Executive format payments

Bi-weekly payment options

For students who need more flexibility in managing tuition, EduConnect USA offers bi-weekly payment plans that let you pay in manageable installments. It is particularly useful if you are enrolled in a work and study program, because your payments line up with the income from your job.

The usual way

Large sums, paid upfront

  • A big lump sum due before each term begins
  • Savings drained, or a loan taken out to cover it
  • Interest and financing charges stacked on top
  • Financial stress running alongside your coursework
  • No connection between what you earn and what you owe
The executive format

Small installments, every two weeks

  • Tuition broken into smaller payments made every two weeks
  • No large sums upfront
  • Interest-free - not a loan, no financing charges
  • Payments aligned with the income from your part-time work
  • Tuition and living expenses managed together, not against each other
The goal is simple: help you manage both tuition and living expenses in a way that complements your academic journey rather than competing with it. Students joining our MS in Healthcare Analytics, for example, can pay for the program as they earn both a salary and their degree.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What international student scholarships are available?
International students can take advantage of several financial aid options through EduConnect USA, including scholarships, grants, and flexible payment plans. These solutions are designed to help reduce the financial burden of studying in the U.S., offering various ways to manage your tuition and living expenses.
How do the flexible payment plans work for international students?
Our bi-weekly payment plans allow international students to spread out tuition payments over the course of their studies, making it easier to manage education expenses without upfront costs.
Can I work while studying and still receive financial aid?
Yes. Many of the programs offered include Curricular Practical Training (CPT) options, allowing you to work while studying. You can also qualify for U.S. scholarships for international students in addition to your work-study income.
Are grants available to international students who work during their studies?
Yes, grants are available even if you're working through CPT or other work-study programs. EduConnect USA provides scholarships for international students enrolled in any executive format program.
Is the payment plan actually interest-free?
Yes. The executive format payment plan is interest-free. It is not a loan and there are no financing charges - your remaining balance after the grant is simply divided into smaller installments paid every two weeks.
How do I find out how much I qualify for?
Speak with an EduConnect USA advisor. Grant eligibility depends on the program you choose and your academic background, so an advisor reviews your transcript, confirms which grant applies, and shows you your exact bi-weekly payment before you commit to anything. Start with a free consultation.
