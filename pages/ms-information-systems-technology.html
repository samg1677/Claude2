Executive MS in Information Systems & Technology

A STEM master's designed around your visa and your job

The Executive Master of Science in Information Systems Technology is delivered through EduConnect's partnership with Alliant University and built for international professionals who cannot afford to stop working for two years. It is 33 credits, takes 12-16 months, and runs in a hybrid format: coursework online, on campus only twice per 8-week term in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Because the degree is STEM-designated and Day 1 CPT eligible, you can begin paid U.S. employment in your very first term and continue working on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate. Tuition is paid in small, interest-free installments, and a $5,500 grant is available.

Our CareerConnect team helps you secure paid off-campus roles, and our advisors handle the part most students find hardest; documents, deadlines, the I-20, and keeping your F-1 status clean while you work.

How an 8-week term works

Built around a full-time job, not against it

Choose your track

Two concentrations, one STEM degree

You build the same core in information systems, data infrastructure, and enterprise technology, then specialize in the direction your career is heading.

Concentration 01

Cybersecurity

As organizations move toward IPv6 and dual-stack networks, legacy IPv4 exposure keeps creating costly vulnerabilities - ARP spoofing, man-in-the-middle attacks, and network reconnaissance among them. This track builds the practical security skills to find and close those gaps, and earns you an accredited cybersecurity master's.

Concentration 02

Technology Management

Learn to bridge the gap between technical teams and business goals: leading digital transformations, managing complex IT infrastructure, and running high-level technology projects. Built for students heading toward IT leadership, consulting, or enterprise architecture.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of jobs can I get with an MS in Information Systems Technology?
Graduates work as computer network architects, systems analysts, information security analysts, cybersecurity specialists, IT project managers, solutions architects, and cloud or infrastructure engineers. The program develops critical thinking and technical literacy that transfer well beyond the IT department - marketing, finance, and operations teams all need people who understand the systems their work runs on.
How do I make my tuition payments?
Program costs are divided into small, interest-free installments paid roughly every two weeks. Once you pay the initial registration fee, the remaining balance is split automatically, so you pay manageable amounts while you study and work. All executive-format programs also offer scholarship opportunities for international students.
What concentrations are offered?
Two: Cybersecurity, focused on securing networks and infrastructure against modern threats, and Technology Management, focused on leading digital transformation and managing complex IT infrastructure. Both are designed around current industry demand and leadership roles in technology.
How does the hybrid schedule work?
Most coursework is completed online. You come to campus twice each 8-week term, in Week 1 and Week 8, and those sessions can be attended in Los Angeles or San Diego. Between them, students can study and work from any U.S. state.
How long does it take to complete the program?
The Executive MSIST is a 33-credit program that typically takes 12 to 16 months. After graduation, STEM OPT eligibility allows up to three additional years of U.S. work authorization.
Can I really work from my first term?
Yes. The program is Day 1 CPT eligible, so once your CPT is authorized you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start, and continue on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate.
