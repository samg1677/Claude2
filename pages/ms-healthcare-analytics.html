Executive MS in Healthcare Analytics

A STEM master's designed around your visa and your job

The Executive Master of Science in Healthcare Analytics is delivered through EduConnect's partnership with Alliant International University and built for international professionals who cannot afford to stop working for two years. It is 33 credits, takes 12-16 months, and runs in a hybrid format: coursework online, on campus only twice per 8-week term in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Because the degree is STEM-designated and Day 1 CPT eligible, you can begin paid U.S. employment in your very first term and continue working on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate. Tuition is paid in small, interest-free installments, and a $5,500 grant is available.

Our CareerConnect team helps you secure paid off-campus roles, and our advisors handle the part most students find hardest; documents, deadlines, the I-20, and keeping your F-1 status clean while you work.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of jobs can I get with an MS in Healthcare Analytics?
Graduates work as healthcare data analysts, clinical managers, health data scientists, insurance and payer analysts, healthcare consultants, nursing home administrators, and health informatics specialists. The degree also suits practicing pharmacists, physicians, and nurses who want to lead with data, and it transfers into federal and state government agencies.
How do I make my tuition payments?
Program costs are divided into small, interest-free installments paid roughly every two weeks over two years. After your down payment and initial registration fee, the remaining balance is split automatically, so you pay manageable amounts while you study and work.
How does the hybrid schedule work?
Most coursework is completed online. You come to campus twice each 8-week term, in Week 1 and Week 8, and those sessions can be attended in Los Angeles or San Diego.
What is the HIMSS partnership and how does it benefit me?
The Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society has designated Alliant an Approved Education Partner, recognizing the Executive MSHA for teaching current, real-world skills. Graduating from a HIMSS-approved program strengthens your credential with employers, and HIMSS members save 15% on tuition.
How long does it take to complete the program?
The Executive MSHA is a 33-credit program that typically takes 12 to 16 months across eight terms. After graduation, STEM OPT eligibility allows up to three additional years of U.S. work authorization.
Can I really work from my first term?
Yes. The program is Day 1 CPT eligible, so once your CPT is authorized you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start, and continue on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate.
