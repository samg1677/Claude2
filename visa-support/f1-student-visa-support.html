What Is F1 Visa Support?

Guidance from admission to approval

The F-1 visa lets international students enroll full-time in an accredited U.S. degree program. Getting it means choosing an eligible program, receiving a Form I-20, filing the right application for your situation, and staying in status the whole way.

EduConnect USA is the official international enrollment partner of Alliant University's School of Management & Leadership. Our F1 visa support walks you through every step, so you can focus on your degree and your career.

  • Free review of your current visa
  • Admission and Form I-20 guidance
  • Application and document prep
  • Day 1 CPT and work placement

FAQ

F1 visa support questions

What does EduConnect USA's F1 visa support include?

Our F1 visa support includes a free case review, help choosing an eligible graduate program, admission and Form I-20 guidance, preparation of your change of status, SEVIS transfer or DS-160 application, interview preparation for applicants abroad, and Day 1 CPT work guidance once you start your degree.

Can I change my status to F-1 without leaving the U.S.?

In many cases, yes. Eligible J-1, B-1/B-2, H-1B, H-4 and other visa holders can apply to USCIS for a change of status to F-1 with Form I-539 while they are still in valid status. We review your situation first to confirm the right path. Read the F-1 change of status guide.

Can I change from a J-1 visa to an F-1 visa?

Many J-1 holders can. If you are subject to the two-year home residency requirement (212(e)), you generally need a waiver before changing status inside the U.S. We check your DS-2019 before you apply. Learn more about J-1 to F-1.

Can I change from a B-1/B-2 visitor visa to F-1?

Yes. You can request a change of status to F-1 from inside the U.S., but you cannot start full-time classes until USCIS approves it, so your program start date needs to be planned carefully. Learn more about B-1/B-2 to F-1.

What is a SEVIS transfer?

A SEVIS transfer moves your F-1 record from your current school to a new one, so you can switch programs without leaving the U.S. or applying for a new visa. Learn more about SEVIS transfers.

Is F-1 a good alternative if I wasn't selected for the H-1B?

For many professionals, yes. An F-1 graduate degree lets you stay in the U.S. legally, keep working in your field through Day 1 CPT, and qualify for OPT and the STEM OPT extension after graduation.

Can I work while studying on an F-1 visa?

Yes, with authorization. Through Alliant University, eligible students can use Day 1 CPT from their first term. After graduation, students may qualify for 12 months of OPT, plus a 24-month STEM OPT extension in STEM-designated programs. See graduate programs.
