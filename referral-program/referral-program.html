FAQ

Referral program questions

Who can join the EduConnect USA Referral Program?

Applicants who will be enrolling in a program offered by EduConnect USA and students who are currently enrolled can join the Referral Program.

How much do I earn for each referral?

For each successful referral you earn one credit equal to two biweekly payments of the program your friend enrolls in: $784 for a Master's program ($392 × 2) or $1,300 for the DBA program ($650 × 2).

When do I need to submit the Referral Form?

You must submit the Referral Form and accept the Terms and Conditions before your friend completes registration with EduConnect USA. Late submissions and proof of earlier conversations are not accepted.

When do I receive my referral reward?

Your credit is confirmed once your friend starts their program, and earned credits are paid out by the end of your friend's first academic term. If you have an outstanding program balance, credits are applied to your remaining biweekly payments instead of being paid in cash.

Can I refer friends before I start my own program?

Yes. You can make referrals and earn credits while you are enrolling, but you must start your own program before you can cash out. Credits are forfeited if you do not enroll in or start your program.

How do milestone bonuses work?

You earn extra credits at 3, 7, 10, 15 and 20 successful referrals: 1, 1, 2, 2 and 3 additional credits. Milestones are counted separately for each program, so Master's and DBA referrals cannot be combined.

1. Eligibility to Participate as a Referrer

To be eligible to enroll as a referrer in the EduConnect USA Referral Program ("Referral Program"), an individual must be an applicant who will be enrolling in a program offered by EduConnect USA or a student who is currently enrolled in the program.

2. Eligibility for Referral Rewards

A referrer is eligible to receive a referral reward when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  1. The Referrer submits the designated Referral Form and acknowledges acceptance of these Terms and Conditions before the Referee completes registration with EduConnect USA. Late submissions of the Referral Form and/or any alternative documentation, including proof of prior communication between the Referrer and the Referee, regardless of when such communication occurred, will not be accepted under any circumstances.
  2. The Referee is a new applicant who has no prior record of registration, enrollment, or an existing application with EduConnect USA at the time the referral is submitted.
  3. The Referee must not have been referred to, introduced to, or assisted with the registration process by any other applicant, student, agent, representative, or third-party referral source. The Referrer must be the sole referral source and may assist the Referee throughout the registration process. It is the Referrer's sole responsibility to verify and ensure that the Referee has not previously worked with, and is not currently working with, any other referral source.
  4. The Referee enrolls in and commences the program offered by EduConnect USA in partnership with Alliant University.

3. Reward Amount and Distribution

  • For each successful referral, which must independently satisfy all eligibility requirements set forth in Section 2, the Referrer will earn credit equal to two biweekly payments applicable to the program in which the Referee enrolls. Master's Program: one credit is $784, equivalent to two biweekly payments of $392 each. DBA Program: one credit is $1,300, equivalent to two biweekly payments of $650 each.
  • The referral reward will be credited after EduConnect USA confirms that the Referee has commenced the enrolled program.
  • All referral credits earned will be paid out to the eligible Referrer by the end of the Referee's first academic term. However, if the Referrer has an outstanding program fee balance, the earned referral credits will be applied toward the Referrer's remaining biweekly payments instead of being paid out in cash.
  • An individual may make referrals while in the process of enrolling in a program offered by EduConnect USA and may earn referral credits during that period. However, a referrer must commence their own program before becoming eligible to cash out any earned referral credits. If the referrer does not ultimately enroll in or commence the program, any referral credits earned before the referrer's program commencement will be forfeited.
