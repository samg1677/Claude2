Your questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

My J-1 visa ends in a few months. Can I stay in the U.S.?
Yes, you may have options. One of the most common paths is to apply to a graduate program and change your status from J-1 to F-1 (student), a process called a Change of Status (COS). With a Day 1 CPT program, you can also keep working from your first day of classes. Request a free consultation and we'll walk you through whether you qualify.
I'm an Au Pair. Do I have to go home after my program ends?
Not necessarily. Many former Au Pairs continue their journey in the U.S. by enrolling in an accredited graduate program. We help you understand the Change of Status process, choose a program and line up paid work, so you can stay, study and keep building your life here.
I came on a tourist visa (B1/B2). Can I become a student?
In many cases, yes. You'd typically apply to a university, receive your I-20, and then file a Change of Status from B1/B2 to F-1. Timing and details matter, so the best first step is a free consultation. We'll review your situation and tell you honestly whether it's a fit.
Can I keep working and study at the same time?
Yes, that's exactly what Day 1 CPT is designed for. Students in our programs can be authorized for CPT from day one, so you can keep your job (or start a new one) while taking classes on a hybrid schedule.
What's the difference between CPT and OPT in plain English?
CPT (Curricular Practical Training) is work authorization during your studies, as part of your program. OPT (Optional Practical Training) is work authorization after you graduate. You get 12 months of OPT, and with a STEM-designated master's you can extend it by another 24 months, for up to 3 years of work in the U.S. after graduation.
What if I already have an employer?
That makes things easier. We help you transition your current job so it's compliant with your CPT authorization, so you can keep working with the same employer from day one of your program.
What if I don't have a job yet?
No problem. Our CareerConnect team works with employer partners across the U.S. to match students with paid positions before their program starts, in a role that aligns with your degree.
Will using Day 1 CPT affect my OPT eligibility later?
If you use 12 or more months of full-time CPT, you become ineligible for OPT at that degree level. Our advisors help you plan your CPT carefully, so you protect both your work authorization during school and your OPT (plus the STEM extension) after graduation.
