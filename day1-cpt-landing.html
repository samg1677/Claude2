FAQ

Day 1 CPT Questions, Answered

Is Day 1 CPT legal?
Yes. Your school's Designated School Official authorizes CPT when practical training is a required part of your program and directly related to your major. Choose an accredited, SEVP-certified school that documents why CPT is integral to the curriculum.
Is Day 1 CPT still allowed after SEVP's August 2026 guidance?
Yes. SEVP's August 2026 broadcast messages did not change the CPT regulations. They tightened how SEVP reads the requirement that CPT be integral to the curriculum, so schools must show that practical training is required for everyone in the program.
Can I keep OPT if I use Day 1 CPT?
Yes, if you keep full-time CPT under 12 months in total for that degree level. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT ends OPT eligibility for that degree, while part-time CPT of 20 hours a week or less does not count toward the limit.
I wasn't selected in the H-1B lottery. Can Day 1 CPT help?
Often, yes. If you're on OPT or a STEM extension, enrolling in an accredited Day 1 CPT program lets you stay in F-1 status and keep working in your field while you plan your next H-1B attempt.
When can I start?
There are six start dates a year: January, March, May, July, August and October, so the next term is never more than about three months away.
Do I need a job offer before I enroll?
No. Many students enroll before they have a CPT job lined up. Your job must relate directly to your major, and CareerConnect helps you find a qualifying placement.
Does EduConnect charge for its services?
No. Consultation and application support are free for students, and application fees are waived.
