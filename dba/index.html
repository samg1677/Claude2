What is a DBA?

The highest professional degree in business.

A Doctor of Business Administration is the highest professional degree in business. Unlike a traditional PhD, which focuses primarily on academic research, a DBA is designed for experienced professionals who want to solve real business problems and lead organizations.

MBA to DBA Pathway

Four clear steps from your MBA graduation to the doctorate.

  1. 1

    MBA Graduation

    Complete your Fast-Track courses with a B or higher and let your Program Director know you are interested in Fast-Track.

  2. 2

    OPT

    Work for up to 12 months full-time and pay off your MBA program fees.

  3. 3

    Register for the DBA

    Register 3–6 months in advance to secure your seat.

  4. 4

    DBA Work & Study

    Continue your CPT and study for 3 more years in the USA.
