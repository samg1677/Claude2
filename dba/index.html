What is a DBA?

The highest professional degree in business.

A Doctor of Business Administration is designed for experienced professionals who want to solve real business problems and lead organizations. Unlike a traditional PhD focused on academic research, the DBA turns your MBA into applied, executive-level expertise.

  • Become a recognized expert and prepare for executive leadership
  • Build consulting credentials and increase long-term earning potential
  • Extend your stay in the USA for 4 additional years while you work and study
