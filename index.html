Major changes are coming to the F-1 student visa system, and international students need to understand what they mean for their education and future in the U.S.

Join EduConnect USA on September 25th for an important conversation with an experienced attorney from VisaPro, following the implementation of the new Duration of Status rule. We'll break down the latest changes and what they could mean for F-1 students navigating CPT, OPT, STEM OPT, program extensions, and continuing on to a new degree.

With the rule taking effect on September 15th, this webinar will give students an opportunity to understand the changes and ask questions directly to an immigration attorney.
