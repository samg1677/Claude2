How it Works

What is Day 1 CPT and How Does it Really Work?

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is a work authorization that lets F-1 students gain paid, off-campus employment directly related to their field of study. Alliant University authorizes students to begin CPT right from the first day of the program — also called Day 1 CPT. During the program you can work part-time or full-time, and in the year after graduation you can continue working full-time on OPT (and STEM OPT for eligible degrees).

Common Day 1 CPT Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Day 1 CPT?
Day 1 CPT (Curricular Practical Training) is a work authorization available to F-1 students enrolled in graduate programs where practical training is an integral part of the curriculum from the start. Unlike most programs that require a one-year waiting period, Day 1 CPT programs authorize paid, off-campus employment from the first day of enrollment.
What is the difference between CPT and OPT?
CPT (Curricular Practical Training) is work authorization during your academic program — it's tied to your enrollment and must relate to your field of study. OPT (Optional Practical Training) is work authorization after you complete your degree. You can receive up to 12 months of OPT, and STEM-designated programs qualify for an additional 24-month extension.
Can I work full-time on Day 1 CPT?
Yes. Students enrolled in these programs are authorized for full-time CPT (more than 20 hours per week) from day one. You can work full-time while taking your courses on the hybrid schedule.
What if I already have an employer?
If you already have an employer or a current position, that's perfectly fine. We help you transition your employment to be compliant with your CPT authorization, so you can continue working with the same employer from the first day of your program.
What if I don't have a job yet?
Our CareerConnect team works with employer partners across the United States to match students with paid positions before their program starts. We help you find a role that aligns with your degree program and career goals, so you know exactly where you'll be working before your first day of class.
Does using full-time CPT affect my OPT eligibility?
If you accumulate 12 or more months of full-time CPT, you become ineligible for OPT. Our academic advisors help you plan your CPT usage carefully to protect your OPT and STEM OPT eligibility after graduation.
How do I find a job that qualifies for CPT?
Your CPT position must be directly related to your major field of study. CareerConnect helps you identify qualifying positions, and your designated school official (DSO) confirms CPT eligibility for each role before authorization is issued.
