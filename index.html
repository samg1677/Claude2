Everything You Need to Know about EduConnect USA

Who is EduConnect USA?

A Los Angeles–based international student consultancy with over 15 years of experience in higher education and 25 years in cultural exchange. We help you choose the right program, enroll, relocate to the U.S., and connect with the right employer.

Talk to an Advisor

What is Day 1 CPT?

Curricular Practical Training that lets eligible F-1 students work in their field from the start of their program.

Visa support

Guidance on F-1 student visas and changing status from J-1 to F-1.

Scholarships

Over $1 million in scholarships awarded to make a U.S. degree more affordable.

Career services

Internship placement, resume assistance and connections to employers.
