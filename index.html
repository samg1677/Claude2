What is Day 1 CPT?

Curricular Practical Training that begins on the first day of your academic program, rather than after two completed semesters.

1

Immediate Authorization

Approved students can work full-time for a U.S. employer from the start of their first semester — no one-year waiting period.

2

Integrated With Studies

Your employment must be an integral part of an established curriculum directly related to your major area of study.

3

Maintain F-1 Status

You remain on an F-1 visa and continue making normal academic progress while gaining U.S. work experience.

Who is Eligible?

Day 1 CPT is typically offered by select graduate programs. You generally need to meet all of the following.

✓

Hold a valid F-1 visa and SEVIS record

✓

Be enrolled in a graduate program (Master's or Doctoral) that offers Day 1 CPT

✓

Have a job offer or signed offer letter from a U.S. employer

✓

The role must be directly related to your field of study

✓

Maintain full-time enrollment and good academic standing

✓

Receive DSO authorization on your I-20 before starting work

How It Works

From application to first paycheck — here's the typical path.

Apply & Get Admitted

Apply to a graduate program that offers Day 1 CPT and receive your acceptance and I-20.

Secure Employment

Obtain a job offer in a role tied to your program's curriculum and core competencies.

Enroll in CPT Course

Register for the required experiential / cooperative education course each term.

Get DSO Authorization

Your Designated School Official issues a new I-20 with CPT authorization on the back.

Start Working

Begin employment on or after the CPT start date listed on your I-20.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Day 1 CPT programs and authorization.

Is Day 1 CPT legal?

Yes. CPT is authorized under 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i). However, programs offering it from day one are scrutinized by USCIS, and authorization must be tied to a legitimate, established curriculum. Always work with an accredited institution.

Will Day 1 CPT affect my OPT eligibility?

Using full-time CPT for 12 months or more eliminates your OPT eligibility. Part-time CPT (20 hours or less per week) does not affect OPT. Always confirm with your DSO before accepting an offer.

Which schools offer Day 1 CPT?

A number of universities — primarily in business, computer science, and analytics graduate programs — offer Day 1 CPT. Choose an institution that is SEVP-certified and regionally accredited.

Can I change employers on Day 1 CPT?

Yes, but each new role requires fresh CPT authorization from your DSO. You cannot begin work for a new employer until a new I-20 with the updated authorization is issued.

Do I still need to attend classes?

Absolutely. Day 1 CPT requires you to be enrolled full-time and maintain satisfactory academic progress. Most programs combine weekend or evening classes with full-time weekday work.
