Start your U.S. career on day one.

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) authorized from your first day of enrollment — designed for graduate programs that integrate professional employment as a core component of the curriculum.

01

Immediate authorization

No 12-month waiting period. Begin working full-time at a U.S. employer the day your semester starts.

02

Curriculum-integrated

Your job is an integral part of an established cooperative-education or experiential-learning program.

03

Maintain F-1 status

Stay enrolled full-time, make satisfactory academic progress, and keep all the benefits of your F-1 visa.

04

OPT-compatible

Part-time Day 1 CPT preserves your 12-month OPT eligibility. Plan carefully with your DSO.

How it works.

From application to first paycheck — the typical path for an F-1 student using Day 1 CPT.

01

Get admitted

Apply to a SEVP-certified graduate program that offers Day 1 CPT and receive your I-20.

02

Secure an offer

Obtain employment in a role directly related to your program's field of study.

03

Enroll in CPT

Register for the required cooperative-education or experiential-learning course each term.

04

DSO authorization

Your DSO issues a new I-20 with CPT authorization printed on page 2.

05

Start working

Begin employment on or after the start date listed on your CPT authorization.

Common questions.

Quick answers to the questions students ask most about Day 1 CPT.

Is Day 1 CPT legal? +

Yes. CPT is authorized under 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i). However, programs that offer it from day one are scrutinized by USCIS — authorization must be tied to a legitimate, established curriculum at an accredited institution.

Will it affect my OPT eligibility? +

12 months or more of full-time CPT eliminates OPT eligibility. Part-time CPT (≤ 20 hrs/week) does not affect OPT. Always confirm details with your DSO.

Which universities offer Day 1 CPT? +

A select group of SEVP-certified universities offer Day 1 CPT, typically in graduate business, computer science, and analytics programs. Choose only regionally accredited institutions.

Can I change employers during the program? +

Yes — but each new role requires fresh CPT authorization from your DSO. You cannot begin work for a new employer until a new I-20 with updated authorization is issued.
