About this webinar

The new Duration of Status rule took effect on September 15th. International students need to understand what it means for their education and their future in the U.S.

Join EduConnect USA on September 25th for a conversation with an experienced attorney from VisaPro. We'll break down the latest changes and what they could mean for F-1 students navigating CPT, OPT, STEM OPT, program extensions, and continuing on to a new degree — then take your questions live.

  • What changes for OPT and the STEM OPT extension
  • Program extensions: when you need one and how it works
  • Moving on to a new degree under the new rule
  • Deadlines you should be tracking right now
