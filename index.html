Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Day 1 CPT

What you need to know about working while studying on an F-1 visa.

What exactly is Day 1 CPT?

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is an F-1 visa benefit that allows you to work in a position directly related to your major. "Day 1" means the program is structured so that CPT is an immediate, integral part of the curriculum — you don't have to wait a full academic year before you're eligible. You can begin working as soon as you enroll.

Is Day 1 CPT legal?

Yes. CPT is authorized under federal immigration regulations (8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)). It's a legitimate part of F-1 status when the practical training is an integral component of the established curriculum. Alliant University is SEVP-certified and regionally accredited — its CPT programs meet all USCIS requirements.

Can I work full-time with Day 1 CPT?

Yes. You can be approved for either part-time (20 hours/week or less) or full-time CPT, depending on your program and schedule. Most students in Alliant's executive-format programs work full-time while attending classes on a bi-weekly hybrid schedule.

Will using full-time CPT affect my OPT eligibility?

If you accumulate 12 months or more of full-time CPT at a given educational level, you are ineligible for OPT at that same level. Many students structure their CPT usage carefully, and our advisors help you plan the best approach. Part-time CPT does not count toward the 12-month limit.

What's the difference between CPT and OPT?

CPT is work authorization you use during your studies. OPT is work authorization available after graduation. STEM-designated programs qualify for a 24-month OPT extension, giving you up to 3 years of post-graduation work authorization in the U.S. Both are F-1 visa benefits.

How much does the program cost?

A $6,000 scholarship is available, and tuition is structured on a flexible bi-weekly payment plan — so you can earn and pay as you go. There are no large upfront tuition costs. Contact our advisors for detailed pricing by program.

Can EduConnect USA help me find work?

Yes. In addition to CPT authorization, EduConnect USA provides career coaching, résumé support, and connections to employers who regularly hire international students. Over 515 students have been placed in paid positions through our network.
