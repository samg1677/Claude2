Frequently asked questions

What does the scholarship include?
The scholarship is an exclusive opportunity to take part in international education programs. Once the winner picks their destination, the EduConnect team will provide details about course duration, city and school options for redeeming the scholarship. Note: the scholarship does not cover Work & Holiday Visa fees in Australia. Once the prize is offered, the winner has 5 business days to accept it; otherwise it's considered declined.
When will I know if I'm the winner?
If you're the winner, we'll contact you no later than the third week of September. We'll also announce it publicly on our social channels. Make sure your email and phone are correct so we can reach you!
What's the prize, depending on whether I'm a client or not?
If you're not yet an EduConnect client and haven't booked a course before the deadline, you'll receive one of the available $3,000 USD scholarships, deducted from your tuition. If you're already a client with pending payments, the prize is a tuition reduction of up to $6,000 USD.
How can I multiply the value of the scholarship?
Book your EduConnect experience before September 15 to be eligible for a tuition reduction of up to $6,000 USD.
