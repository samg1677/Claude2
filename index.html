Everything You Need to Know about EduConnect

The basics

What is EduConnect?

EduConnect is an education advising service that helps international students find, apply to, and enroll in the U.S. programs that fit their goals, background, and budget — with a real advisor guiding every step.

Talk to an Advisor

How do I choose the right program?

We compare programs by career path, major, tuition, start date, and format so you can decide with confidence.

What does the application involve?

A step-by-step walkthrough of documents, deadlines, and requirements — we keep track so nothing slips.

Does it cost anything?

Consultation and application support are free for students. We'll also point you to available scholarships.

How fast can I get started?

Most students hear back from an advisor within one business day and can begin applying right away.
