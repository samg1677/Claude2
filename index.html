About This Webinar

The rule took effect September 15. Here’s what students need to know.

📅 Friday, September 25, 2026 🕐 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM PT 💻 Online (Zoom)

Major changes are coming to the F-1 student visa system, and international students need to understand what they mean for their education and future in the U.S.

Join EduConnect USA on September 25th for an important conversation with an experienced attorney from VisaPro, following the implementation of the new Duration of Status rule.

We’ll break down the latest changes and what they could mean for F-1 students navigating CPT, OPT, STEM OPT, program extensions, and continuing on to a new degree.

With the rule taking effect on September 15th, this webinar gives students an opportunity to understand the changes and ask questions directly to an immigration attorney.

What We’ll Cover

You’ll Learn

  • ✓ What the end of Duration of Status actually changes for F-1 students
  • ✓ Fixed admission dates: what they mean for your I-20 and your I-94
  • ✓ How the new rule affects CPT eligibility and timing
  • ✓ What changes for OPT and the STEM OPT extension
  • ✓ Program extensions: when you need one and how the request works
  • ✓ Moving on to a new degree level under the new rule
  • ✓ Transfers and changes of program — what to watch for
  • ✓ Travel, re-entry, and maintaining status with a fixed end date
  • ✓ Deadlines and timelines you should be tracking right now
  • ✓ Common mistakes that can put your status at risk
  • ✓ How to plan your education and work authorization strategy from here
PLUS Live Q&A with the attorney
