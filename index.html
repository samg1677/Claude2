Common Day 1 CPT Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

What is Day 1 CPT?

Day 1 CPT (Curricular Practical Training) is a work authorization available to F-1 students enrolled in graduate programs where practical training is an integral part of the curriculum from the start. Unlike most programs that require a one-year waiting period, Day 1 CPT programs authorize paid, off-campus employment from the first day of enrollment.

What is the difference between CPT and OPT?

CPT is work authorization used during your studies. OPT is post-graduation work authorization. STEM-designated graduates qualify for an additional 24-month OPT extension on top of the standard 12 months — up to 3 years of post-grad work in the U.S.

Can I work full-time on Day 1 CPT?

Yes. You may be approved for either part-time (≤20 hrs/week) or full-time CPT depending on your program and term. Most students in Alliant's executive-format programs work full-time.

What if I already have an employer?

Perfect — bring them with you. Once enrolled, your CPT authorization can be tied directly to your existing role, as long as the work relates to your field of study.

What if I don't have a job yet?

Our CareerConnect team sources internships and roles with our employer network and supports your search before classes begin. Many students secure a paid position before their first day.

Does using full-time CPT affect my OPT eligibility?

If you accumulate 12 or more months of full-time CPT at the same education level, you become ineligible for OPT at that level. Your advisor will help you structure CPT usage to preserve OPT eligibility.

How do I find a job that qualifies for CPT?

The position must relate to your degree field. Our placement team reviews offers and helps you confirm CPT eligibility before you accept.
