Everything You Need to Know about Day 1 CPT

The basics

What is Day 1 CPT?

Day 1 CPT is Curricular Practical Training that starts in the first term of a graduate program. It is possible when practical training is a required part of the curriculum and directly related to your major. Your school's Designated School Official authorizes it on your I-20.

Read the Day 1 CPT guide

Is Day 1 CPT still allowed in 2026?

Yes. SEVP's August 2026 messages did not change the CPT regulations; they tightened how schools must show CPT is integral to the curriculum.

How do I choose a Day 1 CPT program?

Look at accreditation, real attendance requirements, schedule flexibility and career support, not just tuition.

Can I keep OPT if I use Day 1 CPT?

Yes, if full-time CPT stays under 12 months for that degree level. Part-time CPT does not count toward the limit.

FAQ

Day 1 CPT Questions, Answered

Is Day 1 CPT legal?
Yes. CPT is authorized by your school's Designated School Official when practical training is a required part of your program and directly related to your major. Choose an accredited, SEVP-certified school that documents why CPT is integral to the curriculum.
Which programs offer Day 1 CPT through EduConnect?
Alliant University's MBA, MS Data Analytics, MS Information Systems Technology, MS Healthcare Analytics, DBA and PhD in Organizational Leadership, offered in a hybrid format from Los Angeles and San Diego.
When can I start?
There are six start dates a year: January, March, May, July, August and October, so the next term is never more than about three months away.
Can I keep OPT if I use Day 1 CPT?
Yes, if you keep full-time CPT under 12 months in total for that degree level. Part-time CPT of 20 hours a week or less does not count toward the limit.
Does EduConnect charge for its services?
No. Consultation and application support are free for students.
