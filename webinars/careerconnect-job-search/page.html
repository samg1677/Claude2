What does it actually take to find a job in the U.S. as an international student?

Join us for a live conversation featuring CareerConnect, student speakers, and members of our team as we discuss the real experiences and challenges international students face when searching for employment in the U.S.

You'll also hear directly from students about their experiences, what they've learned, and how CareerConnect has supported their career journey.

Join us live to hear real student experiences, ask questions, and learn how you can make the most of CareerConnect.
