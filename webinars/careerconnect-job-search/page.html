How To Find A Day 1 CPT Job As An International Student

Day 1 CPT lets eligible F-1 graduate students use Curricular Practical Training from their first term, but you still need the right job before you can start working. Here's the path most students follow, and what we'll unpack with real examples in the webinar.

  1. Know The CPT Rules First

    Your job has to be directly related to your program of study, and your school's DSO must authorize it on your I-20 for that employer and those dates before you start.

  2. Target Roles In Your Field

    Focus on positions that match your degree. Most schools ask for an offer letter describing the role, hours and location to approve CPT.

  3. Prepare For U.S. Hiring

    Use a U.S.-style resume, practice interviews, and be ready to explain that CPT is authorized by your school, not a visa the employer sponsors.

  4. Get Support From CareerConnect

    CareerConnect helps you prepare, connects you with employers and guides you through the hiring process, so you're not doing it alone.

Good to know: Day 1 CPT eligibility and requirements are set by your school. Always confirm with your DSO before accepting an offer or starting work.

Webinar FAQ

What is a Day 1 CPT job?

Day 1 CPT refers to Curricular Practical Training that some graduate programs authorize from your first term, instead of after a full academic year. A Day 1 CPT job is a position that is directly related to your program of study and that your school's Designated School Official (DSO) authorizes on your I‑20 for that specific employer and dates.

How do I find a Day 1 CPT job?

Start by confirming your program offers Day 1 CPT and what your school needs for approval, usually an offer letter describing the role. Then target jobs that match your field, prepare a U.S.-style resume and practice interviews, and be ready to explain to employers that CPT is authorized by your school, not a visa they sponsor. CareerConnect helps with each of these steps, and this webinar walks through them with real student examples.

Who is this webinar for?

No. CareerConnect helps you prepare and connects you with employers, but employers make the hiring decisions and individual results vary. Whether you can work, and for how long, depends on your immigration status.
