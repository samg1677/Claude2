Quick Answers

Your Questions, Answered

Can I really work while studying?

Yes. Day 1 CPT and OPT eligibility let you take paid, off-campus roles from the start of the program — turning your studies into real U.S. work experience and income.

Do I have to live in California?

No. The program is hybrid — you can live and work in any U.S. state. You only attend 2 in-person days per 8-week term at the Los Angeles or San Diego campus.

Is the degree accredited?

Yes. Alliant University is accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission — the same body that accredits USC, UCLA, and UCSD — with additional ACBSP accreditation for all business programs.

What does it cost, and is help available?

A $6,000 grant is available to lower tuition, plus an interest-free, bi-weekly payment plan so cost never becomes a barrier. Request information for full tuition details.

I'm on a J-1, B-1/B-2, or F-1 visa — can I join?

Yes. The executive format provides a clear path for current visa holders and SEVIS transfers to continue working and studying in the U.S. Our team guides you through change-of-status options.

When can I start?

There are six start dates each year: January, March, May, July, August, and October — so you can begin sooner rather than later.
