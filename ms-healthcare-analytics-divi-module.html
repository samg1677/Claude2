Good to Know

MS in Healthcare Analytics FAQs

What kind of jobs can I get with an Executive MS in Healthcare Analytics?

Beyond working as a healthcare analyst, graduates pursue roles such as clinical manager, health data scientist, and insurance analyst. The degree also supports pharmacists, physicians, and nurses who want to master data, and prepares healthcare consultants, nursing home administrators, and analysts within federal and state government agencies.

How do I make my tuition payments?

We divide program costs into small, interest-free payments made roughly every two weeks over two years. After your down payment and initial registration fee, our system automatically splits the remaining balance so you can pay manageable installments while you study and work.

How does the hybrid schedule work?

You complete most coursework online and come to campus twice each 8-week term — once in Week 1 and once in Week 8. You can attend these sessions in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Is the program HIMSS-approved?

Yes. Alliant University is a HIMSS Approved Education Partner (AEP), and the Executive MSHA is offered as a HIMSS-approved program. HIMSS members also save 15% on tuition for these master's and doctoral programs.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The MS in Healthcare Analytics is a 33-credit program that typically takes about 12 months across eight terms (up to 16 months). After you graduate, you gain STEM OPT eligibility, allowing you to keep working in the U.S. for up to three additional years.

Can I work while I study?

Yes. As a STEM-designated program with Day 1 CPT, you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start of your program, then continue on STEM OPT for up to 3 years after graduation.
