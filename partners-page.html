Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Who can become an EduConnect USA partner?

EduConnect USA partners with accredited universities, host employers who offer internships and work placements, accreditation and quality bodies, and international education agents. If you help students study, gain authorized work experience, or build careers in the United States, there is a place for you in our network.

What does EduConnect look for in an academic partner?

We look for institutions with recognized accreditation, strong graduate outcomes, and programs that welcome international students. Our academic partner, Alliant University, is a professional practice university in San Diego that specializes in graduate programs and builds from a foundation of inclusiveness.

Is Alliant University accredited?

Yes. Alliant University holds accreditation from the WASC Senior College and University Commission, a regional accrediting body recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. In addition, its School of Management and Leadership is accredited by the ACBSP, covering programs such as the PhD in Leadership, DBA, MBA, and MS in Data Analytics.

How do host employers benefit from partnering with EduConnect?

Host employers connect with motivated international students who are ready to contribute through CPT and OPT placements. We handle the introductions, help keep the placement compliant, and support both the student and the employer through the hiring process.

How do I start a partnership with EduConnect USA?

Reach out through our contact page or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us whether you are a university, an employer, or an education agent, and our team will follow up to discuss how we can work together.

EduConnect USA connects students with partner institutions and employers. Admission decisions are made by universities, employment decisions are made by employers, and accreditation is granted by the relevant accrediting bodies. For questions about a specific program or your individual status, consult the institution directly or a licensed advisor.
