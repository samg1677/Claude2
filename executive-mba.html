About Alliant University

An Accredited Degree You Can Trust

Alliant recognizes the unique challenges international students face when researching and enrolling in U.S. programs — which is why they’ve named EduConnect USA their official enrollment partner for admissions, visa applications, and work placement.

Alliant University is a private institution focused on preparing students for professional careers through a hands-on, practice-oriented approach. With campuses in Los Angeles and San Diego, Alliant offers business degrees that emphasize multicultural perspectives and lasting global impact.

Accreditation

Alliant is accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission — the same body that accredits USC, UCLA, and UCSD.

All business programs hold additional accreditation from the ACBSP (Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs), assuring high academic standards.
