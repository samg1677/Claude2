Leadership

Guided by people who've lived it

  • Rodrigue Colaianni

    CEO & Founder

    With 27 years of experience in the U.S., Rodrigue leads EduConnect in building international partnerships and opening doors for students who never thought studying in the U.S. was possible.

    More about Rodrigue

    Rodrigue also serves as President of Vatel USA Hospitality and Management School, where he helped establish its U.S. campuses and continues to oversee academic programs. Previously, he founded and led International Trainee Network (ITN) and co-founded Odyssey International, facilitating J-1 internship opportunities for over 1,600 international students per year. His leadership is driven by a commitment to advancing global education through strategic innovation, collaboration, and expanding access to opportunity.

  • Jessica Record

    President

    With over 20 years in education through teaching, program design, and internationalization, Jessica builds the programs and processes that help international students reach their goals in the U.S.

    More about Jessica

    Jessica specializes in creating programs and processes that empower international students to reach their education goals in the United States, and she is committed to making education dreams a reality for aspiring global scholars.

  • Aya Kaiho

    Chief Financial Officer

    Aya came to the U.S. as an ESL student on an F-1 visa. She started her career through OPT, just like our students, and has built a 15+ year career in accounting.

    More about Aya

    To pursue her goal of succeeding in the U.S., Aya studied both Business and Accounting at college in Los Angeles. After graduating she began working through OPT, and since then she has built a successful career in accounting spanning more than 15 years. She knows the student journey first-hand.
