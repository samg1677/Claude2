What work opportunities are available during my studies?

Our programs offer access to work opportunities through Curricular Practical Training (CPT), allowing you to gain practical, hands-on experience in your field while you complete your degree.

How can EduConnect USA help with my visa?
  • Expert guidance for F-1 and J-1 visas
  • Support through every step of the process
  • Maximize your chances of staying in the U.S.
Can I find a program that lets me stay in the U.S. after graduation?

Yes! Many of our programs are designed with visa opportunities in mind, offering pathways like CPT and OPT that allow you to work and gain experience while studying and after completing your degree.

What should international students look for in a U.S. university?

Focus on institutions that offer strong academic programs with practical training opportunities and globally recognized degrees.

Also look for universities in locations that provide access to global job markets, offer student-friendly environments, and have a robust support network for both personal and professional development. Programs that emphasize career readiness — such as internships or hands-on learning — help you gain the experience needed to succeed.

How do I choose the right program for work and study?

EduConnect USA offers programs that combine academic study with practical work experience. Our expert advisors help you select the program that aligns with your career goals, ensuring you can work while studying in the U.S.

  • Gain international experience while you study
