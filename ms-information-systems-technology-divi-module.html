MS in Information Systems & Technology

A STEM master's designed around your visa and your job

The Master of Science in Information Systems & Technology is delivered in partnership with Alliant International University and built for international professionals who cannot afford to stop working for two years. It is 33 credits, takes 12–16 months, and runs in a hybrid format — coursework online, on campus only twice per 8-week term in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Because the degree is STEM-designated and Day 1 CPT eligible, you can begin paid U.S. employment in your very first term and continue working on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate. Tuition is paid in small, interest-free installments, and a $5,500 grant is available.

How an 8-week term works

Built around a full-time job, not against it

Each term runs eight weeks. You are on campus at the beginning and the end — the rest is online, on your schedule.

Week 1

On campus

Open the term in person with faculty and your cohort in Los Angeles or San Diego.

Weeks 2–7

Online & working

Complete coursework online around your job. This is where CPT employment and your classes reinforce each other — the systems, networks, and security you study are the ones you are working on all week.

Week 8

On campus

Close the term in person with presentations, assessments, and cohort sessions.

Choose your track

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of jobs can I get with this degree?
Graduates work as computer network architects, systems analysts, information security analysts, cybersecurity specialists, software engineers, developers, programmers, and technology product managers — across technology, finance, healthcare, and government, as well as in the marketing, finance, and operations departments of non-tech companies.
How do I make my tuition payments?
Program costs are divided into small, interest-free installments paid roughly every two weeks. After an initial registration fee, the remaining balance is split automatically, so you pay manageable amounts while you study and work.
How does the hybrid schedule work?
Most coursework is completed online. You come to campus twice each 8-week term — in Week 1 and Week 8 — and those sessions can be attended in Los Angeles or San Diego.
What concentrations are available?
Two: Cybersecurity, an accredited track covering dual-stack IPv6/IPv4 transition, ARP spoofing, man-in-the-middle attacks, and network reconnaissance; and Technology Management, focused on leading digital transformation and managing complex IT infrastructure.
How long does it take to complete the program?
It is a 33-credit program that typically takes 12 to 16 months. After graduation, STEM OPT eligibility allows up to three additional years of U.S. work authorization.
Can I really work from my first term?
Yes. The program is Day 1 CPT eligible, so once your CPT is authorized you can take paid, off-campus roles with U.S. employers from the start — and continue on STEM OPT for up to three years after you graduate. Our CareerConnect team helps you find the placement.
