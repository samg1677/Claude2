Is This Service Right for You?

Make sure Change of Status is the correct option for your situation before applying.

✓ This Service is For You If:

  • You are in the U.S. on a B1/B2 visitor visa
  • You are on J-1 (without 2-year home requirement, or with a waiver)
  • You hold another non-immigrant visa and want to study
  • Your current status is valid (not expired)
  • You want to become F-1 without leaving the U.S.
  • You have been (or will be) admitted to a SEVP-certified school

✗ This Service is NOT For You If:

  • You are already on F-1 status (you may need a Transfer)
  • Your current visa or status has expired
  • You are out of status in the U.S.
  • You entered under the Visa Waiver Program (ESTA)
  • You are on J-1 with a 2-year home requirement (need J-1 Waiver first)

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about EduConnect's Change of Status service.

Are RFE responses really included?
Yes — 100%. If USCIS issues a Request for Evidence (RFE) on your case, EduConnect prepares and guides you through the full written response at no additional charge. This is built into your flat fee. Most consultants charge separately for RFEs, which can add hundreds of dollars. We don't.
How long does Change of Status take?
Standard USCIS processing takes approximately 4–6 months. If you need a faster decision, optional USCIS Premium Processing ($2,075 additional government fee) reduces the decision timeline to approximately 30 business days. We'll guide you on whether premium processing makes sense for your situation.
Can I change status from a B1/B2 tourist visa?
Yes. B1/B2 visitors are eligible to apply for Change of Status to F-1. However, USCIS may scrutinize applications filed shortly after entry, as they look for evidence that you intended to study when you entered. During your consultation, we'll assess your timeline and advise you on the strongest approach.
Can I study while my application is pending?
It depends on your current visa type. B1/B2 holders generally cannot enroll in school until their Change of Status is approved. Holders of certain other visa types (F-2, H-4, etc.) may be able to begin studying once their application is pending and their program start date arrives. We'll advise you specifically during your consultation.
Can I travel outside the U.S. while my application is pending?
No. Leaving the U.S. while your Change of Status application is pending will be treated as abandonment of the application. If you need to travel, you would instead need to apply for an F-1 visa at a U.S. consulate or embassy abroad and re-enter on that visa.
What if my B1/B2 status expires during processing?
If your I-539 was filed before your authorized stay expired, you are in a "period of authorized stay" while USCIS adjudicates your case — meaning you can remain in the U.S. legally. Filing before your status expires is critical, and we'll make sure your case is submitted on time.
Online vs. paper filing — which is required?
If you're filing alone (no dependents), you may file online for $420. If you have co-applicants — a spouse or children who also need to change status — paper filing is required and costs $470. We'll confirm the right filing method for your family situation.
What is EduConnect's success rate?
EduConnect maintains a 99.5% approval rate on Change of Status cases. Our thorough document review process, proactive RFE handling, and experienced team mean your case is set up for success from day one. You can read our client reviews on Google.
What school will I be admitted to?
We work with a network of SEVP-certified schools to find the best fit for your academic goals and schedule. During your consultation we'll discuss programs, costs, location preferences, and English proficiency requirements so you're enrolled in a school that genuinely fits your needs.
